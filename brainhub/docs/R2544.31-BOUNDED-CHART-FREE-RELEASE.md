# R2544.31 - bounded chart context and free evidence routing

Office's selected numeric chart overlays now travel in the protected JEV packet for main decisions, position management and BURST preauthorization. The diagnostic mirror retains the actual transmitted key levels, volatility, readout, level map and liquidation history. Office distinguishes the current reconstruction from a historical decision packet.

BURST uses the normal closed-candle projection and cached chart context. Missing/stale chart context cannot authorize an entry. Background warming is limited to one symbol per 120 seconds through the existing Binance rate governor; the 1-second watcher remains WebSocket based. Repeated preauthorization of unchanged phase/direction waits 120 seconds; a changed phase/direction can be reviewed after 30 seconds. Review verdicts are journaled. Existing LONG/SHORT symmetry, risk checks, position ownership and JEV authority remain enforced.

OpenRouter discovers text models whose complete listed pricing is zero, refreshes the catalog every 30 minutes, constrains provider token prices to zero, and checks account free-request quota every 15 minutes. Without a usable account counter, the conservative local limit is 50 requests per UTC day and 10 per minute. Fallback attempts count toward the limits. Health, quota and cooldowns survive restarts. A removed model waits 24 hours; 429/auth failures stop the shared route rather than spraying fallbacks. Only a recent latest successful reply is healthy.

Worker responses retain line breaks and complete JSON. Truncated or schema-invalid responses cannot qualify as usable evidence. Validated research still requires fetched allowlisted sources and JEV approval before becoming a reference. GitHub references remain engineering references, never market votes. Unverified topic retries wait 15 minutes, successful source fetches are cached for 24 hours, and source-host 429 responses honor Retry-After.

9Router uses the discovered OpenCode catalog, prioritizes recent successful routes, limits evidence fallback to two attempts, and persists health/cooldowns. Text worker/research schema validation precedes a successful health mark. It cannot replace JEV final authority.
