# R2544.17 Decision Quality Research Notes

Purpose: improve JEV's evidence quality and measured learning without importing third-party trading rules or creating a new execution authority.

## References reviewed

- microsoft/qlib (MIT): market-dynamics / rolling research / production separation. Adopted idea: learn outcomes by market regime and keep research evidence separate from live execution authority.
- online-ml/river (BSD-3-Clause): incremental online learning and concept-drift interfaces. Adopted idea: recent sequence/session context is weighted as fresh evidence instead of assuming lifetime behavior is stationary.
- nkaz001/hftbacktest (MIT): tick/order-book replay, latency and queue-aware validation. Adopted idea: microstructure and execution quality are evidence dimensions; do not treat indicator backtests as sufficient proof of executable edge.
- AgentJDrew/backtest-guard (MIT): Deflated Sharpe, PBO/CSCV, purged K-fold, leakage checks. Adopted idea: no measured pattern is promoted into a hard rule merely because it looks good in-sample.
- landtml/purgedcv (MIT): purging/embargo and CPCV for overlapping financial labels. Adopted idea: future offline model validation must separate overlapping outcome windows.
- freqtrade/freqtrade (GPL-3.0): trade-result analytics and backtest/dry-run discipline. Adopted idea: entry reason, exit reason, lane and result must be traceable together.
- nautechsystems/nautilus_trader (LGPL-3.0): deterministic event-driven research/live parity. Adopted idea: the same deterministic features that explain a live decision must be reproducible after close.

## R2544.17 changes derived from the research

1. Sequence learning: detects quick cross-symbol rotation after wins/losses, same-symbol direction flips, two-symbol ping-pong, and when one loss gives back the previous win.
2. Regime-conditioned learning: closed trades are grouped by aligned/conflicting 5m+15m trend and side relation, with stretch/chase context when available.
3. Candidate sequence advisory: before a new JEV decision, compact recent-60m P&L, symbol-switch count, loss streak, peak-to-current realized giveback, quick-switch-after-loss and return-to-recent-symbol flags are supplied as soft context.
4. Entry market signature now stores chart-readout stretch/chase/squeeze/displacement/exhaustion state so later lessons can compare like with like.
5. Office desk cards explicitly separate live last-60m funnel from lifetime closed-trade contract performance.
6. 9Router health telemetry distinguishes FAILED from UNTESTED after cooldown and reports attempt/success/failure counts.
7. Curated OSS registry expanded with Qlib, River, hftbacktest, backtest-guard and purgedcv as read-only engineering references.

## Explicit non-goals / safety boundaries

- No third-party strategy signal is copied into live trading logic.
- No GitHub repository can place an order or override JEV.
- Learning remains soft context, not a hard veto, leverage increase, or self-modifying strategy.
- JEV remains the final strategic decision owner; code after JEV remains mechanical execution/safety.
- Research references do not enter the 52 kB JEV packet as raw documents.
