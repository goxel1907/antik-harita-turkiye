# R2544.38 — closed-bar FVG lifecycle evidence

The useful addition from the Smart Chart / AlgoHK screenshots is FVG lifecycle evidence. This release implements its own deterministic OHLC algorithm; no AlgoHK script, proprietary code, trained weights, or live TradingView dependency was integrated. The screenshots do not establish the original indicator's exact formula.

## Observations and limits

- A three-candle gap becomes available after the third candle closes. Its creating candle is never counted as a test. Processing assumes the engine's closed-candle input; no future candle is read.
- Each gap retains its exact low, high, CE50 and creation time. `fillPct` is deepest observed penetration into the zone, not a probability. Actual high/low overlap is required; a candle wholly beyond the zone can invalidate it without fabricating a touch, fill or midpoint contact.
- Consecutive overlapping candles are one visit. A completed candle outside the zone followed by another overlap starts a separate test. OHLC cannot resolve multiple visits inside one candle.
- States are UNTESTED, TOUCHED, PARTIAL, CE50_TESTED, FILLED and INVALIDATED. A wick reaching the far edge fills the gap; a close beyond the far edge invalidates it. A retired gap stops accumulating observations and cannot silently return as a fresh gap. Legacy `filled` means inactive and is also true for invalidated gaps; lifecycle state distinguishes why.
- Reaction is the observed directional close displacement from the entry-side edge. Excursions start on subsequent completed candles; first-touch candle extrema are excluded because their ordering relative to contact is unknown. A new visit resets reaction observations. This is neither a forecast nor a success score.
- The previous origin lookback of 25 candles and 0.1 ATR small-gap filter remain. Frames expose at most three active zones and two recent terminal zones. This is bounded recent evidence, not a lifetime registry of every FVG.

## Data path and learning

The engine, worker context, JEV numeric packet, protected serialized core truth, mirror digest and Office share lifecycle observations. Full 5m/15m core frames carry active and terminal zones; other timeframes carry compact nearest-zone context. Fib, OTE, OB, trend, liquidity and liquidation mechanisms remain in place.

Entry cases deep-copy exact FVG evidence. Compact entry cases and measured winner/loser analogs retain lifecycle fields; PASS-1/PASS-2 optional memory compaction preserves up to three lifecycle cohort rows. Closed-trade cohorts distinguish first and repeated tests, contain both profitable and losing observations, and do not invent missing historical entry facts. Cohorts may overlap and show association, not causal attribution or model training. External/manual exits retain external authority.

JEV/worker instructions define these measurements as evidence only. No new entry permission, automatic strategic veto, fixed profit target, leverage change or risk-limit bypass is added. No provider or exchange call is needed to calculate lifecycle.
