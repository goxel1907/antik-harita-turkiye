# R2544.32: causal rejection and Office/JEV trend parity

R31 protects selected numeric chart levels but can discard pivot trend geometry during request compaction. It recognizes wick sweeps, but does not retain a separately measured closing-break failure. R32 adds closed-candle evidence for each of the nine existing frames and protects a compact numeric trend/rejection trace in chartOverlayLevels.

## Detection boundaries

- 1m, 3m, 5m, 15m, 30m, 1h and 4h run the same causal rules; 1d also runs them. 45m remains synthetic closed 15m aggregation, never an independent vote.
- Before each candidate candle, freeze prior-20 extremes, confirmed swing pivots and equal levels. Two right-hand candles must already be closed before a pivot becomes a reference.
- A wick crossing and closing back through the frozen level is WICK_SWEEP_REJECTION. A close beyond that level followed by a close back within three candles is CLOSE_BREAK_FAILED.
- Noise tolerance is max(prior ATR14 * .025, prior close * .00001). This is an explicit heuristic, not a calibrated probability. Consider the most recent six starting candles; retain up to four latest events, report omitted counts, age, original break/return times and stillInside status.
- These observations do not predict every fake break or guarantee a reversal. No new automatic gate, signal score, leverage/margin clamp or execution authority is introduced. Existing execution integrity remains binding; JEV remains the strategic authority.

## Trend lines

Retain the existing last-two confirmed HL support / LH resistance definition. Preserve exact anchor and projected prices and actual candle timestamps. Validate all intervening closes, not only the final close: a previously broken or body-crossing line stays inactive even after price recovers. Inactive lines are not drawn. This is a pivot-reference method, not a universal/best-fit trendline search; two contacts do not prove a tradable trend.

## Transport and Office

chartOverlayLevels stores exact from/to/projected price/time, active state, confirmation/invalidation and recent rejection evidence for every frame. Redundant absolute indexes/source prose are omitted from the initial transport digest; no numeric coordinates are rounded. The protected-core hash includes this object, and post-trim mirrorDigest retains it. Main PASS1/PASS2, BURST and management descriptions explicitly tell JEV to weight age and stillInside as observations, not automatic veto/exit.

Office separates current preview from last historical JEV delivery. It shows rejection and active/broken trend rows, including the last transmitted trace; old decisions without R32 fields are explicitly marked. API is additive and read-only; existing APK v9.5.121-r2544.30-office-burst stays unchanged.

## Open-source comparison (04 October 2026)

Methods reviewed, no imported code/dependency or new provider request:

- [smart-money-concepts](https://github.com/joshyattridge/smart-money-concepts): roughly 2k stars; confirmed swing/closing-break distinction. [Look-ahead issue](https://github.com/joshyattridge/smart-money-concepts/issues/101) reinforces confirmation delay in historical replay.
- [PyIndicators sweep implementation](https://github.com/coding-kitties/PyIndicators/blob/master/pyindicators/indicators/liquidity_sweeps.py): wick and break/retest distinctions, delayed pivots.
- [pytrendline](https://github.com/ednunezg/pytrendline): checks body crossings and configurable tolerance; its exhaustive fitting/score is not used as trading authority.
- [Quantum Algo SMC](https://github.com/quantumalgo-official/smart-money-concepts-indicator): one star observed; confirmed-bar sweep comparison.
- [BTC-LiquiditySweeps](https://github.com/CryptoArtisty/BTC-LiquiditySweeps/blob/main/index.html): zero stars observed; recent-three-bar sweep example. Uses all returned klines including potentially forming bars and integer zone rounding; unsuitable as a drop-in source of exact closed-candle truth.
- [LiquiditySweepEA](https://github.com/mrmolli12/LiquiditySweepEA): zero stars observed; repository/license inventory inspected, no claim of validating its execution engine.

Star count is discovery metadata, not accuracy/reliability. No downloaded scripts were run.
