# R2544.35 — management, mobile status and JEV burst exits

Position-management requests previously duplicated current market frames and compacted immutable position geometry as market context. The protected management request now separates the original entry thesis and management contract from the current canonical market packet. Lossless row encoding retains numerical truth and the existing 48,000-byte ceiling. Essential information that cannot fit still fails closed.

## Partial exits and measured experience

Guard and JEV partial reductions share the original take-profit quantity budget. Pending profit orders are cancelled and rebuilt around confirmed fills, retaining at least 25% of the original size and the larger original runner allocation. Protective stops stay installed. Unknown market acknowledgements are persisted by client order identity and queried before another partial; recovered JEV fills count toward the partial count and cooldown contract. Concurrent profit reductions/restoration/full exits share a symbol-side lock.

Final exit authority is no longer inferred from P&L or a prior TP milestone. New close records separate original quantity, zero remaining quantity after confirmed exchange absence, and last observed quantity. Runner-observed MFE/MAE and capture/giveback enter outcome context. A read-only quality projection marks historical inferred exit labels and missing excursions; raw history and financial totals remain unchanged. Burst experience is separate from fee-reconciled normal trade P&L and survives bounded entry memory compaction. Learning remains soft context; no model weight training, self-modification or automatic promotion is enabled.

## Mobile contract

`GET /live/status?compact=1` preserves LIVE, expiry, configuration, decisions, counters and learning totals while omitting recursive market dossiers. Full status remains available. The paired Android patch isolates critical PC health/status polling from optional account/position/discovery/burst requests. Existing 45-second freshness and three-failure rules remain intact; failure still produces unknown PC state.

Android discovery, external analysis and saved plans remain separate from PC/JEV authoritative plans. The UI labels their source explicitly. Discovery acceptance is shown only after an acknowledged current snapshot, never as JEV approval or an order signal. Android order initiation stays disabled.

## Burst contract and limits

The old global chart refresh throttle was longer than the chart freshness window. Refreshes now use bounded global/per-symbol gates under the existing Binance governor, and the selected candidate is rechecked after warming. Preauthorization, ignition, freshness, spread, L2 integrity, TTL, loss pause and user/exchange leverage limits are not bypassed.

JEV chooses HOLD or EXIT_NOW for sufficient burst profit; the fixed profit target is removed. Independent fast-fail, exchange stop and maximum duration remain mandatory. The protective stop stays installed while a standalone market exit is unconfirmed; unknown fills are queried before retry. Realized burst price-path measurements require a confirmed positive execution price, otherwise remain unmeasured.

**Same-symbol same-side synthetic additions are blocked with `BURST_SHARED_POSITION_NOT_ISOLATABLE`.** Binance merges those lots into one position, so a separate stop/exit cannot atomically guarantee preservation of the core lot during uncertain or partial fills. Independent burst positions remain supported. This is an execution-integrity limitation, not a relaxed strategic trigger.
